Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 · Applies to the Scrapyard Brawl app on iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version

  • Scrapyard Brawl is a single-player game that runs offline. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We, the developer, don't receive it, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Watching a video clip for a reward is always optional. Those clips are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure them. That is the only data collection in the game.
  • Purchases go through Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Scrapyard Brawl is developed and published by Kaizen AI Solutions (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at hello@kaizenaisolutions.com.

Data that stays on your device

The game stores the following only on your phone or tablet, in the app's private storage:

  • Your save: fighters, items, beasts, currencies, progress and settings.
  • Which purchases you own, so the game can unlock them.
  • Your invite code and whether you've redeemed one.

We have no copy of this data and no server that receives it. Deleting the app deletes it. Your device's own backup service (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include it, under that service's terms.

Fight codes and invite codes

The game can create a text code describing one fight (fighters' stats, gear and the fight's random seed) or an invite code. A code is only shared if you copy it and send it yourself. Codes contain no name, account ID or device ID.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. They handle your payment and send the app a confirmation of what you bought. We don't receive your card details, name or billing address. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google (see Support).

Optional video clips (Google AdMob)

Some screens offer a reward, such as extra fuel, for watching a short video clip. You choose each time; nothing plays automatically. The clips are provided by Google AdMob. When a clip is requested, Google may collect and process:

Your choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks permission before any tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, Google can't use your advertising identifier. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: you'll be asked for consent before ads are personalized, through Google's consent message.
  • The “No Clips” purchase: if you own it, the game gives you clip rewards without requesting an ad at all.
  • Not watching clips is always an option. The whole game can be played without them.

Children

Scrapyard Brawl is rated for ages 12 and up and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game in a way that caused data to be collected, contact us and we'll help you reach Google to have it deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, delete or correct personal data, or to opt out of its “sale” or “sharing” for targeted advertising. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data Google collects through ads, use the choices above or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com. If you need help, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Your save is protected by your device's own app sandbox. The game doesn't send it over the internet.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting anything new, such as cloud saves or analytics, we'll update this page and change the date at the top before that version is released.
