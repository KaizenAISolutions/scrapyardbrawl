Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026 · Applies to the Scrapyard Brawl app on iPhone and iPad

The short version

  • Scrapyard Brawl is a single-player game that runs offline. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We, the developer, don't receive it, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It collects no data at all.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Scrapyard Brawl is developed and published by Kaizen AI Solutions (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at hello@kaizenaisolutions.com.

Data that stays on your device

The game stores the following only on your phone or tablet, in the app's private storage:

  • Your save: fighters, items, beasts, currencies, progress and settings.
  • Which purchases you own, so the game can unlock them.
  • Your invite code and whether you've redeemed one.

We have no copy of this data and no server that receives it. Deleting the app deletes it. Your device's own backup service (iCloud) may include it, under that service's terms.

Fight codes and invite codes

The game can create a text code describing one fight (fighters' stats, gear and the fight's random seed) or an invite code. A code is only shared if you copy it and send it yourself. Codes contain no name, account ID or device ID.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment and sends the app a confirmation of what you bought. We don't receive your card details, name or billing address. Refunds are handled by Apple (see Support).

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your phone, such as when your fuel is full or a salvage run is done. They are created on your device and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

Scrapyard Brawl is rated for ages 13 and up and isn't directed at children under 13. The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, delete or correct personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. If you have a question, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Your save is protected by your device's own app sandbox. The game doesn't send it over the internet.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting anything, for example through optional video ads, cloud saves or analytics, we'll update this page and change the date at the top before that version is released.
