Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026 · Applies to the Scrapyard Brawl app on iPhone and iPad

The short version

  • Scrapyard Brawl is a single-player game. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We, the developer, don't receive it, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts or photos.
  • The game offers optional video ads (“clips”) that you can watch for a reward. They are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure them. That is the only data collection in the game.
  • The game may ask whether Google may track you for ads. Saying no costs you nothing: you still get every clip and every reward.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Scrapyard Brawl is developed and published by Kaizen AI Solutions (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at hello@kaizenaisolutions.com.

Data that stays on your device

The game stores the following only on your phone or tablet, in the app's private storage:

  • Your save: fighters, items, beasts, currencies, progress and settings.
  • Which purchases you own, so the game can unlock them.
  • Your invite code and whether you've redeemed one.
  • Anything you type in the game: your fighter's name, battle cry and status, and messages on the Radio and on noticeboards.

We have no copy of this data and no server that receives it. Deleting the app deletes it. Your device's own backup service (iCloud) may include it, under that service's terms.

The other survivors are simulated

Every rival, crew mate and contact in the game is generated on your device. Their replies and Radio chatter are generated on your device too. What you type is never sent to us or to Google, and no other player sees it.

Fight codes and invite codes

The game can create a text code describing one fight (both fighters' in-game names, stats, gear and the fight's random seed) or an invite code. A code is only shared if you copy it and send it yourself. Codes contain no real name, account ID or device ID.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment and sends the app a confirmation of what you bought. We don't receive your card details, name or billing address. Refunds are handled by Apple (see Support).

Optional video clips (Google AdMob)

Some screens offer a reward, such as extra Fuel, for watching a short video ad. You choose each time; nothing plays automatically, and there are no banners or pop-up ads. The clips are provided by Google AdMob.

Google's ad software is part of the app, but the game keeps it switched off until clips are on offer: never the first time you open the game, and not before you finish the starting tasks or reach Level 5. After that it starts when the game opens, so that a clip is ready when you ask for one, and it needs an internet connection. From then on Google may collect and process:

Your choices

  • Tracking permission: when clips first become available, the game may show Apple's “Allow this app to track?” question (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, or you are never asked, Google does not get your advertising identifier, so it can't use it to link what you do in this game with other companies' apps and websites. You still get every clip and every reward. You can change your answer anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent message: where the law requires your consent for ads (for example in the UK, Switzerland and the European Economic Area), Google's consent message asks before any ad is requested. To change an answer you gave there, open Account in the game and tap Ad Choices. That row is only shown to players the consent message applies to.
  • The “No Clips” purchase: a one-time purchase in the Store. If you own it, the game stops showing video ads, gives you the clip rewards straight away, makes no ad requests, and no longer starts Google's ad software when it opens.
  • Don't watch: clips are always optional. The whole game can be played without ever watching one, and it works with no internet connection; only the clips need one.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your phone, such as when your Fuel is full or a salvage run is done. They are created on your device and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Children

Scrapyard Brawl is rated for ages 13 and up and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game in a way that caused data to be collected, contact us and we'll help you reach Google to have it deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, delete or correct personal data, or to opt out of its “sale” or “sharing” for targeted advertising. We, the developer, hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. For the data Google collects through the clips, use the choices above (declining tracking is the opt-out from sharing for targeted advertising) or Google's own tools at myadcenter.google.com and policies.google.com/privacy. If you need help, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Your save is protected by your device's own app sandbox. The game doesn't send it over the internet.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting anything new, such as cloud saves or analytics, we'll update this page and change the date at the top before that version is released.
