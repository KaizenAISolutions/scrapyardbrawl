Purchases

I bought something and it didn't show up

Open the Trader, tap Top Up, then Restore Purchases. This re-unlocks everything you own permanently: No Clips, Unlimited Fuel, 2x Salvage, outfits and the one-time bundles. Chrome and timed items (the Monthly Card, the Season Pass) live in your save on that device and don't come back with a restore. If it still isn't there after a restore, email us with the date of the purchase and your receipt from Apple or Google.

Why didn't Restore give my Chrome back?

Chrome packs are used up when you spend them, like coins, so the stores don't let any game restore them. Only things you own permanently come back with a restore.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by the store you paid, not by us:

  • iPhone and iPad: reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, then choose the purchase.
  • Android: Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or see Google Play's refund page.

Saves

Is my progress saved online?

No. The game is fully offline and saves on your device. That means no account to lose, but also no cloud save yet. If you delete the app or change phones, your progress doesn't come with you unless your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) restores the app's data. Purchases you own permanently can always be restored on a new device.

I have five save slots. Which one is mine?

Each slot is a separate fighter. The game opens the last one you played. Switch slots from the title screen.

Playing

What is Fuel?

Every brawl costs 3 Fuel. Fuel refills by itself, 1 point every 10 minutes, up to 30. When you run out, you can wait, watch a short clip for +10 Fuel (up to 4 times a day), use a Fuel Can, or spend Chrome.

Do I have to watch video clips?

Never. Every clip is optional and gives a bonus. Everything in the game can be earned without them. The “No Clips” purchase skips the video and still gives you the reward.

What are fight codes and invite codes?

After a Pit or Spire fight you can copy a fight code. A friend who pastes it under Friends → Codes watches the exact same fight. Your invite code gives a friend 50 Chrome once. They then get a thank-you code to send back, which gives you 50 Chrome, for up to 10 friends.

How are crate odds decided?

Every crate shows its exact odds. Tap Odds on the crate screen. The Steel Crate also guarantees a top-tier item within 10 openings.

Reporting a bug

Email hello@kaizenaisolutions.com with:

  • your device model and iOS or Android version,
  • the game version (on the game's App Store or Google Play page, under Version or About this game),
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead,
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if you can.

We usually reply within two working days.
