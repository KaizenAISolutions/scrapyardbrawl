Purchases

I bought something and it didn't show up

Tap your Chrome balance on Home to open the Store, then tap Restore Purchases. This re-unlocks everything you own permanently: Unlimited Fuel, 2x Salvage, outfits and the one-time bundles, with everything in the bundle if this save doesn't have it yet. Chrome packs and timed items (the Monthly Card, the Season Pass) live in your save on that device and don't come back with a restore. If it still isn't there after a restore, email us with the date of the purchase and your receipt from Apple.

Why didn't Restore give my Chrome back?

Chrome packs are used up when you spend them, like coins, so the App Store doesn't let any game restore them. Only things you own permanently come back with a restore.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by Apple, not by us: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, then choose the purchase.

Saves

Is my progress saved online?

No. The game is fully offline and saves on your device. That means no account to lose, but also no cloud save yet. If you delete the app or change phones, your progress doesn't come with you unless your phone's own backup (iCloud) restores the app's data. Purchases you own permanently can always be restored on a new device.

I have five save slots. Which one is mine?

Each slot is a separate fighter. The game reopens the last one you played. Switch or delete saves under MENU > Save Slots: tap the save name.

Playing

Which phones can run it?

On iPhone, you need an iPhone XR, iPhone XS or newer (iPhone SE from the 2nd generation on) with iOS 15 or later. Older iPhones such as the iPhone 8 or iPhone X can't install it. On iPad it runs in iPhone mode.

What is Fuel?

A brawl in the Pit costs 3 Fuel (1 while you learn the ropes). Fuel refills by itself, 1 point every 10 minutes, up to 30. When you run out, you can wait, use a Fuel Can, or spend Chrome.

What are fight codes and invite codes?

After a Pit or Spire fight you can copy a fight code. A friend who pastes it under Friends → Codes watches the exact same fight. Your invite code gives a friend 50 Chrome once. They then get a thank-you code to send back, which gives you 50 Chrome, for up to 10 friends.

How are crate odds decided?

Every crate shows its exact odds. Tap Odds on the crate screen. The Steel Crate also guarantees a top-tier item within 10 openings.

Reporting a bug

Email hello@kaizenaisolutions.com with:

  • your device model and iOS version,
  • the game version (on the game's App Store page, under Version),
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead,
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if you can.

We usually reply within two working days.
