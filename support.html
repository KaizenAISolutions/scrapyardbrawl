Purchases

I bought something and it didn't show up

Tap your Chrome balance on Home to open the Store, then tap Restore Purchases. This re-unlocks everything you own permanently: No Clips, Unlimited Fuel, 2x Salvage, outfits and the one-time bundles, with everything in the bundle if this save doesn't have it yet. Chrome packs and timed items (the Monthly Card, the Season Pass) live in your save on that device and don't come back with a restore. If it still isn't there after a restore, email us with the date of the purchase and your receipt from Apple.

Why didn't Restore give my Chrome back?

Chrome packs are used up when you spend them, like coins, so the App Store doesn't let any game restore them. Only things you own permanently come back with a restore.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are decided by Apple, not by us: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, then choose the purchase.

Saves

Is my progress saved online?

No. The game has no account and no server of ours: it saves on your device. That means no account to lose, but also no cloud save yet. If you delete the app or change phones, your progress doesn't come with you unless your phone's own backup (iCloud) restores the app's data. Purchases you own permanently can always be restored on a new device.

I have five save slots. Which one is mine?

Each slot is a separate fighter. The game reopens the last one you played. Switch or delete saves under MENU > Save Slots: tap the save name.

Playing

Which phones can run it?

On iPhone, you need an iPhone XR, iPhone XS or newer (iPhone SE from the 2nd generation on) with iOS 15 or later. Older iPhones such as the iPhone 8 or iPhone X can't install it. On iPad it runs in iPhone mode.

What is Fuel?

A brawl in the Pit costs 3 Fuel (1 while you learn the ropes). Fuel refills by itself, 1 point every 10 minutes, up to 30. When you run out, you can wait, watch a short clip for +10 Fuel (up to 4 times a day), use a Fuel Can, or spend Chrome.

What are fight codes and invite codes?

After a Pit or Spire fight you can copy a fight code. A friend who pastes it under Friends → Codes watches the exact same fight. Your invite code gives a friend 50 Chrome once. They then get a thank-you code to send back, which gives you 50 Chrome, for up to 10 friends.

How are crate odds decided?

Every crate shows its exact odds. Tap Odds on the crate screen. The Steel Crate also guarantees a top-tier item within 10 openings.

Video clips

Do I have to watch video clips?

Never. A clip is a short video ad you can choose to watch for a bonus, such as +10 Fuel or a doubled haul. Nothing plays by itself, there are no banners or pop-ups, and everything in the game can be earned without clips.

I don't see any clip buttons

Clips stay off the first time you open the game and until you finish the starting tasks or reach Level 5. After that the buttons appear on the Fuel prompt, after a brawl, on a salvage haul, at the Trader and on the daily calendar. Each one has a daily limit.

It says “No clip right now”

Clips are the one part of the game that needs an internet connection, and sometimes there is no video to show. Check your connection and try again in a bit. You only get the reward when the video plays to the end; closing it early gives nothing and doesn't use up your daily limit.

What does No Clips do?

No Clips is a one-time purchase in the Store. The game stops showing videos and gives you the clip rewards straight away, with the same daily limits (doubling a brawl haul has no limit). It is yours for good and comes back with Restore Purchases on a new phone.

Why did the game ask to track me?

The clips come from Google AdMob. Apple makes every app ask before an ad company may link what you do in the app with other companies' apps and websites. Say no if you like: you still get every clip and every reward. You can change your answer in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
